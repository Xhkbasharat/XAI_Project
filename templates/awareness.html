Understand Pneumonia Early

Pneumonia is a serious lung infection that inflames air sacs in one or both lungs. Early awareness, prevention and timely medical consultation can save lives.

🫁

What is Pneumonia?

Pneumonia is a lung infection that causes inflammation in the air sacs of one or both lungs. These air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, causing breathing problems, cough, fever and chest discomfort.

Common Causes

• Bacterial infections
• Viral infections
• Fungal infections
• Weak immune system
• Smoking and lung damage
• Untreated flu or respiratory illness

Symptoms

• Fever & chills
• Difficulty breathing
• Chest pain
• Cough with mucus
• Fast breathing
• Fatigue and weakness

Prevention

• Wash hands regularly
• Avoid smoking
• Stay hydrated
• Take vaccinations
• Eat healthy food
• Maintain immunity

Risk Factors

Higher risk in:

• Children
• Elderly people
• Asthma patients
• Diabetes patients
• Smokers
• Weak immunity

Emergency Signs

Seek urgent medical care if:

• Severe breathing issue
• Blue lips or nails
• High fever
• Confusion
• Severe chest pain

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can pneumonia be cured?

Yes. Early diagnosis and proper medical treatment significantly improve recovery.

Is pneumonia dangerous?

It can become serious, especially in children, elderly people and people with weak immunity.

Can vaccines prevent pneumonia?

Vaccinations help reduce risk and are recommended for high-risk groups.
